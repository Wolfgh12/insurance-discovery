What is LegacyTrace?

LegacyTrace is Ghana's centralized legacy registry and encrypted estate system. It allows policyholders to proactively catalog active policies, land titles, and certified wills—ensuring that if unforeseen circumstances arise before informing living relatives, family members can search with full confidentiality to discover what exists and claim their rightful inheritance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how LegacyTrace protects policyholders and simplifies family inheritance.

Can a policyholder hold policies from different insurance companies on one profile?
Yes. LegacyTrace allows policyholders to aggregate life, health, vehicle, and property insurance policies across different underwriting companies into a single encrypted dashboard.
Who is allowed to access digital wills and asset records?
Sensitive documents such as wills and title deeds remain strictly locked and are only accessible by the policyholder or released to verified next-of-kin following authenticated death verification.
How are Group Policies managed?
For corporate or family group policies, the registered Policy Admin has distinct privileges to update beneficiary rosters, oversee renewals, and coordinate claims.
