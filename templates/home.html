Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how mySikaVault protects policyholders and simplifies family inheritance.

Can a policyholder hold policies from different insurance companies on one profile?
Yes. mySikaVault allows policyholders to aggregate life, health, vehicle, and property insurance policies across different underwriting companies into a single encrypted dashboard.
Information regarding sensitive documents, digital wills, and estate records: who is permitted access?
Information regarding sensitive documents, deeds, and digital wills remains locked under AES-256 cryptographic protection. These records are strictly accessible by the policyholder during their lifetime and are released exclusively to verified next-of-kin following mandatory biometric calibration and identity authentication.
How does family policy discovery work using only a Ghana Card ID?
Surviving relatives enter a deceased loved one's Ghana Card ID (GHA-XXXXXXXXX-X) to verify active underwritten coverage. Preliminary searches confirm policy existence while keeping policy values, sums assured, and private documents concealed until formal next-of-kin intake.
Why is a 3-angle biometric facial capture required before unlocking a vault?
To prevent fraudulent liquidation, impersonation, and estate diversion, Ghanaian data and estate regulations require front, left, and right facial verification. Each capture is matched against registered next-of-kin records and permanently logged into the statutory audit vault.
How do claims dispatch to licensed insurance underwriters in Ghana?
Once a claimant is verified and payment is settled, an audited claim docket is dispatched directly to partner claim desks—including Enterprise Life, SIC, StarLife, GLICO, Hollard, and Vanguard Assurance—enabling rapid statutory disbursement without third-party intermediaries.
Can policyholders catalog bank accounts, Treasury Bills, and land property?
Yes. Beyond insurance policies, mySikaVault functions as a comprehensive estate depository where citizens securely record commercial bank accounts, Government of Ghana Treasury Bills, mutual funds, listed GSE shares, land titles, and family lineage trees so assets are never lost or left unclaimed.
How are Group Policies managed?
For corporate or family group policies, the registered Policy Admin has distinct privileges to update beneficiary rosters, oversee renewals, and coordinate claims.
