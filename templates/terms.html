6. Post-Mortem Inactive Account Protocol

To balance individual privacy during life with rightful estate continuity upon demise, mySikaVault operates under a strict four-stage protocol:

Phase A: Lifetime Privacy & Zero-Leakage Search
During the lifetime of a Policyholder, all estate documents, account balances, sums assured, and digital wills remain encrypted under AES-256 protocols and hidden from public search queries. Public searches confirm only whether an underwritten record exists, revealing zero personal asset data.
Phase B: Claimant Authentication Gate (The "Two-Key" Rule)
Following a Policyholder's death, a claimant must satisfy two distinct security factors: (1) Primary Key: successful 3-angle facial biometric verification matching an official Ghana Card; and (2) Secondary Key: claimant credentials must match the pre-registered Next-of-Kin credentials or Family Tree relationships deposited by the Policyholder while living.
Phase C: Identity Discrepancy Escrow & Desk Review
If a Claimant's identity fails to match the deceased's registered records, the automated gateway immediately locks. The docket is moved into Administrative Escrow, requiring the claimant to provide an official Certified Death Certificate from the Births and Deaths Registry and certified true copies of High Court Letters of Administration before manual clearance is evaluated.
Phase D: Subscription Default & Estate Preservation Window
If a Policyholder passes away and their annual subscription lapses, mySikaVault covenants that it will not wipe or purge the records. The vault enters a read-only "Dormant Estate State" for a statutory holding period of not less than seven (7) years from the last renewal, ensuring surviving family members retain the ability to discover their inheritance.
Phase E: Interpleader Shield for Family Estate Disputes
In the event of competing claims, family caveats, or legal challenges between surviving relatives, mySikaVault reserves the unilateral right to freeze the subject vault. The vault shall remain locked without liability until presented with a certified Court Order or joint consent release signed by all disputing factions and validated by a High Court Registrar.
